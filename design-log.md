# Design log

Every design decision, with the evidence behind it. The deck's "how data influenced design" section (rubric criterion 4) is assembled from this file.

| Date | Decision | Evidence (finding IDs) | Alternatives considered | Decided by |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 28 Oct 2026 | Focus on a proactive study app that supports neurodivergent students | Group empathy work and user stories; QOC Design Space Analysis | Student Saviour, SpokeSafe, LectureFlow; socialising, fitness and finance areas | Group meeting |
| 28 Oct 2026 | Build the hi-fi on LearnHarness | Group research after empathising | Building from scratch | Group meeting |
| 29 Oct 2026 | Questionnaire scope moves from problem choice (v1) to studying (v2) | QOC gap: v1 had no lecture or study questions (B4, B5, P3 unanswered) | Keep v1 and add questions | KAYZ |
| 8 Nov 2026 | Drop the observation study (T07). Empathise runs on three methods: autoethnography, questionnaire (n = 22) and 15-minute interviews with volunteers | Observation's unique value is catching what people do but can't say (Wk 3). Our autoethnographies already log behaviour in the moment, and the interviews ask about the most recent real session. Recruiting, ethics and note-taking for 4-6 sessions cost about a week, which pushes Define past the lo-fi deadline | Keep 4-6 observations; cut to 2 observations; observe teammates only | KAYZ |
| 8 Nov 2026 | Questionnaire closed at n = 22. The design leads on *starting and sustaining* a session, ahead of explaining content | Q-1, Q-2, Q-3, Q-11 (`research/questionnaire-results.md`) | Lead on content explanation, the LearnHarness default (rejected: Q-3 says understanding is the rarest problem) | KAYZ, for group review |
| 8 Nov 2026 | Proactive nudges are capped at 1-2 a day and use timetable and lecture materials only, never location | Q-8, Q-9, Q-10 | No cap; location-aware nudges | KAYZ, for group review |
| 8 Nov 2026 | Both teaching modes are offered (worked example first, or questioned through), chosen by the student | Q-7 (preference split 10 vs 8) | Question-led only (AE theme 2) | KAYZ, for group review |
| 8 Nov 2026 | Revisit the 28 Oct topic choice now the data exists. A proactive study app, designed with neurodivergent students in mind, holds. The design targets all students and keeps adaptation per person | Q-1 and Q-2 (starting and sustaining are the top problems), Q-8 (19/22 welcome an unprompted nudge), AFF-A. Q-12 shows no clear neurodivergent difference at n = 22, so a separate ND mode is not supported | The three parked concepts: Student Saviour, SpokeSafe, LectureFlow | KAYZ, for group review |
| 8 Nov 2026 | LearnHarness as the hi-fi base moves from settled to **provisional**, pending a group decision at the Tue 10 Nov sync | Challenged by Q-3 (understanding is the rarest problem, so the engine's strength is our users' smallest need) and AE2-11 (nothing in a session required a separate application), plus AE2-1 (setup cost before any teaching). Supported by AE2-5 and AE2-8 (the confidence rating and thinking field drew the session's only unprompted enthusiasm) | Options A, B and C in `meetings/2026-11-10-sync.md` | KAYZ, for group review |
| 10 Nov 2026 | **The core problem is getting students to start studying, not how they learn.** The design leads on the start moment | Q-1, Q-2, Q-11, AFF-A, convergence C-1. Q-3 says understanding is the rarest problem, picked by 1 of 22, and AE2-10 is a tool that teaches well and was judged to do nothing about starting. Later corroborated by INT-03-a, INT-04-a, INT-05-a, INT-06-a | Lead on content explanation; split the effort evenly between starting and teaching | Group sync, 10 Nov |
| 10 Nov 2026 | **Primary user: a student who struggles to start. Anti-persona: a technical power user like us** | AE2-1 (six setup steps before the first question), AE2-P14 (a 15-question onboarding read as 60 and "harder than actual studying"), AE2-P15 (a correct answer followed by quitting anyway). Q-12 found no clear neurodivergent difference at n = 22, so the design targets all students and adapts per person | Design for the motivated self-starter; build a separate neurodivergent mode | Group sync, 10 Nov |
| 10 Nov 2026 | **Keep LearnHarness's learning engine and cut its setup and overhead.** This is option D in the sync brief, A and C combined | Keep: AE2-5 and AE2-8, the confidence rating and reasoning field, the only part of either walked prototype that drew unprompted praise. Cut: AE2-1 (setup), AE2-9 (measuring before teaching read as an exam), AFF-H (opacity: AE2-2, AE2-4, AE2-7) | Option A, keep LearnHarness whole; option B, rebuild on an LLM API; option C, a scripted teaching loop. All three are set out in `meetings/2026-11-10-sync.md` | Group sync, 10 Nov |
| 10 Nov 2026 | **Most of the build effort goes on getting students to start:** study blocks, nudges and app locking | C-1, Q-1, Q-2, AFF-A for the problem. Q-8 (19/22 welcome an unprompted nudge), Q-9 (1 to 2 a day), Q-10 (timetable yes, location no), AFF-F for the mechanism. LIT-3 frames a scheduled block as an implementation intention | Spend the build on the teaching engine; build both layers evenly | Group sync, 10 Nov |
| 10 Nov 2026 | **Onboarding stays short and comes after a first taste of value** | AE2-P14 and AE2-P2 (cost priced and refused before answering a question), AE2-P1 (a welcome screen that gives no reason to start), AE2-P10 (the one question that changed something visible earned the next one), AFF-G | Keep a full preference questionnaire up front, as in the onboarding spike | Group sync, 10 Nov |
| 10 Nov 2026 | **Roles for the build:** Jiven owns LearnHarness and how users learn; Jishnu owns the lo-fi for getting started | Follows from the two decisions above: the start layer carries most of the effort and most of the evidence, so it gets its own owner | Keep the T01-T30 role split unchanged | Group sync, 10 Nov |
| 10 Nov 2026 | **The product is named LockedIN** | The name states the job the evidence gives it: getting into a study session, and staying in it. C-1 and Q-1 say starting is the problem; Q-2 and AFF-C say sustaining is the second | Names describing the teaching, which Q-3 ranks as the smallest need | Group sync, 10 Nov |
| 10 Nov 2026 | **Second anti-persona: Dev, the technical power user.** The design is not for someone who enjoys configuring a tool | AE2-1 (six setup steps before the first question), AE2-P14 (a 15-question onboarding read as 60, and the observation that the people who finish it have no problem studying), AE2-P15 (a correct answer followed by quitting anyway). Follows the 10 Nov decision that the primary user struggles to start and the anti-persona is a technical power user like us | Keep Sam as the only anti-persona; treat the power user as a secondary persona to support | Group sync, 10 Nov; written up by KAYZ |
