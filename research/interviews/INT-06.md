## Findings
- **INT-06-a:** About a week passed between deciding to study and starting, then two to three hours on the day itself. They kept getting sidetracked, then "just opened the website and started".
- **INT-06-b:** What would help them start: "Absence of distraction." Named distractions: notifications, tidying, opening the curtains.
- **INT-06-c:** A bad session: a library visit with a friend, where they chatted and doom scrolled instead of studying.
- **INT-06-d:** In their last session the phone sat next to them at first; they later put it face down on the bed and studied at the desk. Studying away from their room, such as in a library, helps them focus.
- **INT-06-e:** Their main tool is a notebook-style AI app: they upload question banks and practice tests and it generates quizzes. It's the first study app they've used, and they've never stopped using it.
- **INT-06-f:** They count themselves done when they can answer every practice question with full confidence. They memorise by cumulative repetition (A, then A and B, and so on).
- **INT-06-g:** A 10-minute post-lecture quiz: useful because it covers the lecture, but doing a quiz is annoying and they'd probably put it off ("I'm just lazy to be honest").
- **INT-06-h:** Two fixed weekly study times with the phone locked and breaks every 25 minutes: they don't think they'd use it. If they're already focused the phone doesn't matter; if they can't focus, everything is a distraction. Heavy notification alerts are the exception.
- **INT-06-i:** Shown versus asked depends on the subject: guiding questions step by step in maths; told first, then reverse-engineer, for language-based subjects. They check understanding by explaining to someone who doesn't know it.

## Surprises
- **The sharpest objection to the lock (INT-06-h):** the barrier is getting focused in the first place, and a locked phone doesn't create focus. It supports putting effort on the start moment rather than on the lock.
- **The one app they never abandoned is low-effort to start:** upload the material and the quiz is generated (INT-06-e). Compare INT-02-i: flashcards are only worth it if someone else makes them.
