# Sync, Mon 16 Nov 2026: literature review, sketches and the phone/laptop split

Attendees named in the recording: Jiven, Jishnu, KAYZ. Lekan and Rahul were not named.
Source: the team Notion meeting page, which was summarised from a dictated recording. Speakers weren't labelled, so owners appear only where a name was said. Decisions were confirmed by KAYZ on 18 Nov.

## The narrative agreed
Most students already have the first 80%: they know how to study. Starting is the hard part. The phone gets them started, and the LearnHarness engine does the learning on the laptop.

From the interviews, as discussed: students do start, then hit resistance within about five minutes, from too many resources or resources badly laid out, so doing something else is easier. The group named two levers: remove hurdles to studying, and add hurdles to distraction. It agreed to use both: a phone block for distraction, and short multiple-choice questions to make studying easy.

## Decisions
1. The narrative is getting students started, backed by the LearnHarness engine.
2. One app with phone and laptop versions, synced between devices. Not two products.
3. The phone gets students started; the laptop does the learning (LearnHarness).
4. The literature review and the sketches run in parallel this week.
5. Onboarding stays light. Feature toggles live in Settings and aren't chosen by an LLM.
6. Leaderboards are optional; the interviews were mixed on competition.
7. **Dropped:**
   - the in-lecture confusion tap (the app can't tell where you are in the lecture, and it interrupts note-taking);
   - pulling in Panopto recordings (too heavy);
   - questions built from past papers (most students don't have any);
   - two separate apps (one user base).

| Device | Job | Features |
|---|---|---|
| Phone | Getting started | Calendar blocking with a phone block, notifications (one is itself a single multiple-choice question), leaderboards (can be switched off). No learning machinery beyond that one question |
| Laptop | The learning | The LearnHarness engine: the hi-fi prototype |
| Both | One app | Same account and settings, synced, like WhatsApp and WhatsApp Web |
