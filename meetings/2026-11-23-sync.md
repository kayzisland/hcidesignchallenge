## Decisions
1. Every feature in the deck follows one chain: a screenshot of the running system, what it does, why we built it (QOC, research), and its effect (user testing).
2. The viva deck has at most 12 slides, picked from the main deck.
3. Testing pipeline: lo-fi testing, then recalibrate the lo-fi, then map the changes onto the hi-fi. With about 40 features, changing the hi-fi after every finding would take too long.
4. The ~40 features are cut to a core set: calendar blocking, reminders, app blocking and the LearnHarness engine.
5. A focus group on Fri 27 Nov: 6 to 7 people from mixed subjects.
6. Internal hard deadline: Tue 1 Dec.
7. Daily meetings until the deadline.

## The order problem, as the group agreed to tell it
The hi-fi engine was built before the lo-fi, and research started late compared with the build. The deck's story: we started on the hi-fi engine because early research pointed that way and it was the easiest part to build first. The lo-fi cycle (personas, scenarios, storyboards, sketches) then reshaped what the student sees, and each change carries its own justification.
